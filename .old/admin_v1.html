Vault Admin

Encrypts photos and site content in your browser. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and the PIN is never saved.

1. Enter the PIN

It must be the same PIN the lock screen uses, otherwise the site cannot decrypt what you make here.

2. Add photos

Drop photos here, or click to choose
Files already named like date3-7.jpg are placed automatically. Others get the next free number in the group you pick.

3. Put them in your repo

The zip contains images/NAME.enc files. Unzip it into the root of your repo (merging the images folder), then commit and push. New photos appear on the site on their own.

Site text (the encrypted page body)

This is the HTML of the site itself, kept encrypted in content.enc. Load it, edit the words, then encrypt it again. Photo groups look like <div class="gallery-grid" data-group="date1" data-prefix="date1" data-alt="First date photo"></div>.
